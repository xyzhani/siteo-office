# Logistics rules (Snow Bell enforces these)

Shippers choose forwarders on trust. Accreditations, coverage and track record are claims that must come from the company's own website, word for word.

## Forbidden phrases
- leading
- best
- number one
- #1
- trusted by
- guaranteed
- 100%
- on-time record
- fastest
- cheapest
- worldwide
- global network
- award-winning
- door-to-door anywhere

## Rules
- Never add an accreditation, registration or membership (for example AEO, customs schemes, trade associations, ISO certificates) that the facts don't state exactly.
- Never add ports, routes, countries or lanes beyond the facts.
- No fleet sizes, tonnage, volumes, years in business or customer counts unless they are in the facts.
- No transit times, prices or service levels unless stated.
- Phone, email, address and hours must match the facts exactly.
