# Logistics emails (tone + templates)

## Tone
Plain, professional business-to-business. Short sentences, no hype, no exclamation marks. Respect that they're busy: get to the point in the first two lines. Never mention AI. Under 140 words.

## First email
Subject: A website concept for {business}
Hi {owner_or_team},

I looked at the {business} website and put together a free concept of a modern version, using only the details already on your site:

{concept_url}

A few things the current site is missing:
{issues}

If it's useful, we can build the real site for {price}. No obligation; just reply if you'd like to talk it through.

{signature}

## Deposit email
Subject: Next step for the {business} website
Hi {owner_or_team},

Thanks for confirming. To get started we take a {deposit_percent} deposit ({deposit}), paid securely with PayPal:

{checkout_url}

Once it's paid we'll send the invoice for the balance ({balance}) and start the build.

{signature}

## Reply to a question
Subject: Re: the {business} website concept
Hi {owner_or_team},

Thanks for your reply. The full website is {price}, with a {deposit_percent} deposit to start. The concept is here: {concept_url}

Happy to answer anything else.

{signature}

## Follow-up
Subject: Reminder: invoice {invoice_number}
Hi {owner_or_team},

A short reminder that the balance for your new website ({balance}) is still open. You can pay it with PayPal here:

{invoice_url}

{signature}
