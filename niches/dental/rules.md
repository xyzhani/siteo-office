# Dental rules (Snow Bell enforces these)

Health advertising is regulated in most countries. A concept must only say what the practice itself says.

## Forbidden phrases
- best
- number one
- #1
- top-rated
- guaranteed
- guarantee
- painless
- pain-free
- 100%
- 5-star
- every patient
- leading
- award-winning
- cheapest
- miracle
- permanent results
- risk-free

## Rules
- No prices, discounts or finance offers unless they are in the facts.
- No reviews, ratings or testimonials. We never invent them.
- No before/after claims or promises about results.
- Name only treatments the practice lists.
- Don't call anyone a "specialist" unless the facts say so.
- Phone, email, address and hours must match the facts exactly.
