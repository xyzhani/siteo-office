# Dental emails (tone + templates)

Placeholders in {curly braces} are filled in for you. With an AI key, the agent rewrites each email in this tone using the same facts; without one, the template is used as written.

## Tone
Warm, short, plain English. One person writing to another, not a marketing blast. No hype, no pressure, no exclamation marks in the first email. Never mention AI. Under 140 words.

## First email
Subject: A new website idea for {business}
Hi {owner_or_team},

I looked at the {business} website and made you a free concept of what a modern version could look like, built only from the details already on your site:

{concept_url}

A few things the current site is missing:
{issues}

If you like it, we can build the real thing for {price}. No obligation at all; just reply and let me know what you think.

{signature}
