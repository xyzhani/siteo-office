# Restaurant rules (Snow Bell enforces these)

## Forbidden phrases
- best
- number one
- #1
- award-winning
- michelin
- world-famous
- famous
- legendary
- authentic
- organic
- locally sourced
- 5-star
- guaranteed
- happy diners
- loved by

## Rules
- No prices or offers unless they are in the facts.
- No reviews, ratings, awards or press quotes. We never invent them.
- Only name dishes, menus and dietary options the restaurant lists.
- "Organic", "local" and "authentic" are claims: only if the facts say exactly that.
- Phone, address and hours must match the facts exactly.
