# Restaurant emails (tone + templates)

## Tone
Friendly and relaxed, like a regular who happens to build websites. Short. No hype, no pressure. Never mention AI. Under 140 words.

## First email
Subject: A new website idea for {business}
Hi {owner_or_team},

I had a look at the {business} website and made a free concept of a fresh, phone-friendly version, using only what's already on your site:

{concept_url}

What the current site is missing:
{issues}

If you'd like it, we can build the real one for {price}. No pressure at all; just reply with what you think.

{signature}

## Deposit email
Subject: Next step for the {business} website
Hi {owner_or_team},

Brilliant, glad you like it. To get going we take a {deposit_percent} deposit ({deposit}) through PayPal:

{checkout_url}

Once it's in, we'll send the invoice for the balance ({balance}) and start building.

{signature}

## Reply to a question
Subject: Re: the {business} concept
Hi {owner_or_team},

Thanks for the reply. The full website is {price}, with a {deposit_percent} deposit to start. Here's the concept again: {concept_url}

Happy to answer anything else.

{signature}

## Follow-up
Subject: Quick reminder: invoice {invoice_number}
Hi {owner_or_team},

Just a friendly nudge that the balance for your new website ({balance}) is still open. You can pay it with PayPal here:

{invoice_url}

{signature}
