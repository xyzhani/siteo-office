How it works

  1. Misso finds businesses with a website
  2. Tom audits them and shortlists the weakest
  3. Knight builds a concept from real facts only
  4. Snow Bell fact-checks every line, sends it back if needed
  5. Sherlock notes what the old site misses
  6. Nemo writes the email; you approve it
  7. Scrapper reads the reply and sends a PayPal deposit link
  8. Fluffy invoices the balance with PayPal and follows up

Runs

Nothing is sent until you approve it. Edit anything first.

Each agent's instructions are plain text in agents/*.md. Change how they write, what they check, what they never say. This is teaching, not training: no fine-tuning, it takes effect on the next run.
